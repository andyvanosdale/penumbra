**What this wave can and cannot show (read first).** Eleven dev years of monthly observations at
a 5% monthly tracking volatility give a standard error of about 44 bps a month. The bar below
(dev z at least 2.0) therefore needs a true premium near 10 points a year to pass with reasonable
probability; the literature's long-only expectation for these signals is 2 to 5 points, which
passes about 4% of the time. A null here is written `null (underpowered below N pp)` and is not
evidence of absence. What the wave fixes as ledger facts are the point estimates with their
standard errors, the turnover and cost drag, the survivorship diagnostics, and whether anything
in this family deserves the longer, delisting-complete history that would add power.

##### Hypotheses (one per signal; the catalog's expected gross / net at base)

- **2a 12-1 momentum (long).** The top decile of the eligible universe by the return from
  session D−252 to D−21 (the last month skipped), bought at the next open after each month-end
  and held one month, beats the equal-weight eligible universe by 3 pp a year gross, +1 pp net of
  the base schedule (one-way turnover about 42% a month). Prior: Jegadeesh and Titman; the
  long-only free-data expectation is small and the 2009 start puts the 2009 momentum crash just
  outside dev.
- **2b 52-week-high proximity (long).** The top decile by `close / max(close over the 250
  sessions ending D)` on split-adjusted closes (George and Hwang), monthly, beats the universe by
  2 pp a year gross, −1.5 pp net (turnover about 85% a month: a new high is a transient state).
- **2d Weekly reversal, liquid names (long the losers).** Among names with a 20-session median
  dollar volume of at least USD 10M, the bottom decile by the 5-session return, bought at the
  next open after each week-end and held one week, beats the floored universe by 15 bps a week
  (7.8 pp a year) gross and −1 pp net (turnover about 85% a week; base drag 8.8 pp). A gross-
  positive, net-negative 2d is a cost-schedule result, not a near miss; the V3 variant (ranked
  on D−6 to D−1, the last session excluded) and the bounce flag separate the one-day bid-ask
  bounce from the reversal.
- **Expected drag, pre-stated** (free-panel one-way turnover: 2a 42% a month, 2b 85% a month, 2d
  85% a week). Base drag: 2a 2.0 pp a year `uncapped` (2.8 `smallcap`), 2b 3.5 (4.7), 2d 8.8
  (17.7 at high); 2d needs 26.6 bps a week gross for 5 pp net. None of the catalog's expectations
  reaches the bar.

##### Survivorship direction per signal (on record before the run)

The listing is the current one (2026-10-01; caps 2026-10-07): the whole history of every name
delisted before 2026-10 is missing. On a top-minus-universe statistic the bias is the two sides'
differential delisting exposure times the dropped names' shortfall. **2a:** undetermined (past
winners and past losers both delist: losers by failure, winners by acquisition). **2b:**
undetermined (same two channels). **2d:** favours the long: the bottom-decile losers of a week
are over-represented among names that later fail, and those names are absent, so the surviving
losers rebound more than the true cohort. The random-slice placebo shares the bias and does not
test it; the lag-250 placebo, the mirror row and the price-matched comparator are the
survivorship controls, and the wave-1 +18 pp price-level artifact is the reference size.

##### Common protocol (locked; identical for every row)

- **Data.** yfinance daily bars, `auto_adjust=False`, 2009-01-01 → 2023-12-31, pulled on this
  machine on 2026-10-07 for the 2026-10-01 Nasdaq Trader common-stock list (4,884 names; 4,156
  with bars); market caps current as of 2026-10-07 (Nasdaq screener export, then yfinance
  `fast_info`). Under `PENUMBRA_DATA_ROOT` (`~/penumbra-data`), never committed. The feature
  panel is the wave-1 panel (`PANEL_VERSION` unchanged); the three new arrays are built from it
  in `build_arrays`. Split actions (`raw/universe/splits.parquet`: ticker, date, ratio new/old)
  are pulled by `pull --splits`, one yfinance request per ticker, resumable, dates only; **if the
  pull has not finished within two hours it is abandoned, the floor diagnostic is recorded as not
  run, and nothing else changes.** Binance 1d klines are absent on this machine: the wave-1 rank
  regression (1d, 1e) is skipped and recorded as such. The holdout (2024-01-01 onward) is never
  downloaded; the pull functions refuse such an end date; the split pull reads dates only.
- **Eras.** Dev 2010-01-01 → 2020-12-31; confirm 2021-01-01 → 2023-12-31. D lies inside the
  era. **Boundary rule (wave 1's, applied literally):** a holding period whose exit fill session
  is after the era's last session is null and is not counted, so no dev statistic reads a confirm
  bar. Consequence, fixed here: the last valid dev rebalance is the one whose *next* D is not the
  era's last session, so dev has 130 monthly observations (D = 2010-01-29 → 2020-10-30, exit fill
  2020-12-01) and 572 weekly (D = 2010-01-08 → 2020-12-18), confirm 34 monthly and 154 weekly;
  the brief's counts (131 / 573 / 35 / 155) include the period whose exit fill is the first
  session of the following era. The pre-run SE and MDE80 below use the brief's 131 and 573; the
  realized counts replace them in every row. Halves split at 2015-07-01 (dev) and 2022-07-01
  (confirm) on D.
- **Universe.** The wave-1 floors on every session D: a bar on each of the 250 sessions ending D,
  median unadjusted dollar volume over D−20..D−1 ≥ USD 500k, `rvol_20` > 40% annualized, split-
  adjusted close on D ≥ USD 2. Lanes: `uncapped` (every eligible name) and `smallcap` (current
  cap < USD 2B). **Only `uncapped` can reach `confirm`; `smallcap` cannot graduate**, because the
  2026 cap mask conditions membership on not having compounded (100 to 350 bps a month on the
  excess, sign undetermined); its rows are run and ledgered as diagnostics. 2d adds
  `med_dv_20_prev` ≥ USD 10M. **Ranked set and comparator** on D: the eligible names with a
  non-NaN score on D (2d: the USD 10M-floored set).
- **Rebalance dates.** 2a and 2b: the last session of each calendar month on the equity session
  calendar (the union of dates with any bar, as wave 1). 2d: the last session of each ISO week
  (Monday to Sunday). Scores use data through D's close.
- **Fills and holding returns.** Entry at `a_open` on the session after D; exit at `a_open` on
  the session after the next D. A name with no bar at the exit session is marked at its last
  `a_close` inside the period (sessions from the fill session to the one before the exit) and
  counted `liquidated`; a name with no bar at the fill session is not filled, drops from the
  basket and the comparator for that period, and is counted `unfilled`. Price basis: every return
  is a ratio within the yfinance adjusted series (`a_open`, `a_close` = raw × adj_close / close);
  `close` (yfinance Close: split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted; "raw" only relative to
  `a_close`) serves only the USD 2 floor, dollar volume and the 2b score.
- **Slice.** k = ⌈n/10⌉ (⌈n/5⌉ for V2) names with the highest (2d: lowest) score, equal weight
  at formation, no intra-period rebalancing; ties at the cutoff score are included and the
  realized k is reported (2b's new highs all score exactly 1.000, so its realized k can exceed
  ⌈n/10⌉). k < 10 is a skipped observation, counted and reported.
- **Comparator.** The equal-weight mean of the same open-to-open return over every name in the
  ranked set on D (2d: the USD 10M-floored set; 2b V3: the full 2b ranked set, for comparability
  with V1; 2a V3: each tranche against its own formation-date ranked set). No cost charged.
- **Cost.** Flat round trip by `med_dv_20_prev` bucket on D (≥ USD 10M: 10 / 20 / 40 bps;
  USD 1M–10M: 20 / 40 / 80; USD 0.5M–1M: 40 / 80 / 160 at low / base / high). Each entering name
  pays its bucket's round trip at entry, staying names pay nothing, and in the first period the
  whole basket enters: cost_t = (Σ entering rt_i) / k_t. One-way turnover by count times the
  round-trip schedule is the whole drag (no factor of 2). One-way turnover τ_t = entering names /
  k_t; annual one-way turnover = mean τ_t × 12 (or 52). 2a V3: the tranche formed at D_t enters
  against the tranche formed three months earlier (the one it replaces), pays at formation, and
  the observation's cost is the new tranche's cost divided by the number of live tranches (the 1e
  construction); its reported turnover is the portfolio's (mean of τ_t / live_t × 12).
- **Statistic.** One observation per rebalance date: d_t = r_port,t − r_uni,t − c_t. Period mean
  m, SE = sd(d_t, ddof 1) / √T, z_plain = m / SE; annualized excess = 12 m or 52 m. z_nw = m /
  √((γ_0 + 2 Σ_{j=1..L} (1 − j/(L+1)) γ_j) / T) with autocovariances at ddof 0, L = 3 monthly,
  4 weekly. **z_gate = min(z_plain, z_nw) decides every gate**; flag `autocorr` when they differ by
  more than 0.3. The trade-weighted mean (Σ k_t d_t / Σ k_t) is reported, never decides; flag
  `size-concentrated` when it differs from m by more than 1 SE. p_dev = 1 − Φ(z_gate), one-sided.
  Pooled z = (z_d √T_d + z_c √T_c) / √(T_d + T_c), from z_gate in both eras. Hit rate = share of
  periods with d_t > 0. Also reported per row: annualized return and volatility of the basket and
  the universe, both maximum drawdowns, k median, eligible-count median.
- **Power statement, next to every z.** The pre-run σ of d_t is locked at 5.0% a month and 2.5% a
  week (free-panel measurements gave 4.8% and 6.3%). Dev SE is then 43.7 bps a month or 10.4 bps
  a week; MDE80 at z = 2 is 14.9 pp a year monthly, 15.4 weekly. A true 5 pp premium gives
  E[z] = 0.95 in dev and passes the two-gate 4.0% of the time; 10 pp passes 21.6%; the null
  0.29% per row. The realized SE replaces σ in every row: `mde80_pp = ppy × 2.8416 × SE × 100`
  (ppy = 12 or 52); a `null` row with mde80_pp above 12 pp is written `null (underpowered below
  N pp)`; the ledger `dev_n` cell reads `<T> mo (MDE80 <N> pp)` or `<T> wk (MDE80 <N> pp)`.
- **Period labels.** The year of a period is the year of its fill session (so "January fills"
  are the periods entered in January, and "calendar-2020 fills" the periods entered in 2020);
  the halves split on D.
- **Scale-by-10 test** (in `pytest -q`): `a_open`, `a_high`, `a_low`, `a_close` and `close` × 10
  with `in_universe`, the cap mask and `med_dv_20_prev` held fixed leaves every `diff_<level>`,
  `port`, `uni` and basket unchanged to 1e-12; the USD 2 floor and the 2b score are checked
  separately (the 2b score is scale-free; the floor is not and is held fixed by construction).
- **Model.** None. Fixed rules. No LLM call, no fitting, no purchase.

##### Placebos, three, each with its own ledger column

- **Random slice.** 200 draws, seeds 20261008 + i (i = 0 … 199), identical for every row and
  era, one `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)` per draw. Each date holds the actual row's realized
  k_t names from the ranked set, keeping a held name with probability 1 − τ_t (the actual row's
  one-way turnover that date) and refilling uniformly from the unheld names of the ranked set; a
  held name no longer in the ranked set is replaced, the replacement paying entry cost; when k_t
  rises the extra slots fill uniformly, when it falls held names drop uniformly with the same
  rng. Order of operations per date, fixed here: drop the held names that left the ranked set;
  draw one uniform per remaining held name and keep it when the draw is ≥ τ_t; if more than k_t
  are kept, drop uniformly to k_t; fill the open slots uniformly from the ranked set less the
  kept names; every filled slot is an entering name. 2a V3 draws one tranche per formation date
  with that tranche's own τ_t; 2b V3 draws from the full 2b ranked set at V3's realized k. Count
  matched, not bucket matched. The draws' per-draw statistics go to `random_<tag>.csv`. **Pass:**
  the actual row's annualized net excess at base ranks at or above 191 of 201 among itself and
  the 200 draws (ascending; at most 10 draws at or above it), else `null`. **Engine check:** the
  draws' median zero-cost z lies within 0.5 of zero and their median annualized excess within
  1 pp of zero, else the wave stops and the cause is found before any table is read as a result.
- **Lag 250.** The score as of D − 250 sessions, with the ranked set and k as of D (no overlap
  with the 12-1 window; 2b V3 lags both scores). It runs on its own valid dates (about 119 valid
  dev months for 2a, from about 2011-01), is exempt from the minimum-period rule and reports its
  valid count. The table prints `actual_on_lag250_dates_z`, the actual row's z_gate on the
  placebo's dates, and the `characteristic` test reads the placebo beside it. **Pass:** the
  placebo's zero-cost z_gate below +2.0, one-sided; a fail is `characteristic` (survivorship is
  one cause, a factor tilt another), sized by the lag-250 annualized excess. A placebo z at or
  below −2 is reported, not failed.
- **Lag 20.** The score as of D − 20 sessions, ranked set and k as of D; reported, decides
  nothing.

##### Planted controls

Two rows at zero cost: +42 bps a month (5.04 pp a year) or +10 bps a week (5.2 pp), and the
legacy +50 bps, added to every d_t. Planted z_plain minus actual z_plain must equal X / SE within
0.01 (X = 42 or 10, not the bar); the bar-sized shift is the empirical power statement: 0.96
z-units at the locked σ (42 / 43.7, 10 / 10.4). Deterministic, never re-run.

##### Mirror slice

The control, not a variant: the opposite end of the same ranking at the same slice size (the
bottom decile or quintile for 2a and 2b, the top decile or quintile for 2d), held long at zero
cost against the same comparator; 2a V3's mirror runs in the same tranches; 2b V3's mirror is the
2b bottom decile among the same top-half-of-2a names. A mirror long row with zero-cost z_gate ≥
2.0 means both ends of the ranking beat the universe, which is what the wave-1 price artifact
looked like: verdict `artifact`, with the mirror's annualized excess recorded as the bias size.

##### Survivorship and look-ahead checks (every row)

- **Price-matched comparator.** For each held name on D, the equal-weight mean of the same
  holding-period return over the ranked-set names in the same within-day quintile of `close` on
  D (quintile q = ⌈5 × percentile rank⌉ among the ranked set); the matched comparator for the
  period is the mean over held names of their quintile means; d^m_t = r_port − r_match − c_t.
  **Pass:** the matched net excess at base has the sign of the unmatched one and is at least
  0.5 × it. Also reported: price-quintile shares of held names per year (`diag`) and
  `price_ratio_top_uni` = median over periods of (median formation `close` of the basket ÷
  median `close` of the ranked set); below 0.5 ties the row to the wave-1 artifact.
- **Vintage table per dev year.** Mean eligible count, mean held count, share of held names
  first seen after the pull's first session (a first bar later than 2009-01-02); reported, not
  gated.
- **Split-adjusted USD 2 floor.** The floor sits on a close back-adjusted through the pull date;
  it admits future reverse-splitters and ejects future forward-splitters. If the split pull
  completes within two hours, `run --floor raw_close` rebuilds `raw_close = close × Π(ratio of
  splits dated after D)`, re-runs the V1 rows on that floor and writes `floor_diag_<tag>.csv`
  (flip share of universe-days per era, net excess under both floors); flag if the net excess
  moves by more than 25%. The locked universe is unchanged either way.
- **Look-ahead test.** The three new arrays (`ret_12_1`, `close_to_max_close_250`, `ret_5`) join
  `test_universe_and_features_at_D_are_unchanged_by_bars_after_D`; `rank_equity` on a panel
  truncated at D gives identical baskets through D; the shift test runs for 2a, 2b and 2d.

##### Bar (rank-mode v2; a new pre-registration, flagged in the ledger)

- **Dev:** z_gate ≥ 2.0 at base; annualized net excess ≥ 5 pp at base and > 0 at high; at least
  120 monthly or 520 weekly valid periods.
- **Confirm:** same sign; z_gate ≥ 1.0; at least 30 monthly or 140 weekly valid periods; pooled z
  ≥ 2.5; net positive in 2 of 3 confirm years; no parameter change. Confirm z is 1.0, not 1.5,
  because 35 months at σ 5% pass a true 10 pp premium 30% of the time at 1.5 (SE 84.5 bps,
  E[z] 0.99) while the pooled gate holds the per-row null pass at 0.29%.
- **Rule 4 (artifact) conditions, every row, evaluated in dev:** net at base positive in 7 of
  the 11 dev years; the sign of the net at base holds in both halves (split 2015-07-01), with the
  top 5% of periods removed (the ⌈0.05 T⌉ periods with the largest d_t at base: 7 months, 29
  weeks; `top5pct_share` = their share of Σ|d_t|, `z_wo_top5pct` = z_gate without them) and with
  January fills removed (`z_ex_jan`); z_gate at base ≥ 1.5 with calendar-2020 fills removed
  (`z_ex2020`), because the free panel puts 2020's contribution to 2a's z at +0.5 to +0.7, which
  a sign condition cannot catch at z 2.0 to 2.6; the table prints `z_contrib_2020` = Σ_{2020
  fills} d_t / (sd × √T) (= 12 × mean_2020 / (sd √T) for a full year); the price-matched
  comparator keeps the sign and at least half the excess.
- **Rule 5:** a fourth variant raises dev z to 2.5 and is flagged. None is planned.

##### Family

Rows that can graduate: 3 signals × 3 variants on `uncapped` = 9. Ledger columns `family_n = 9`
and `p_sidak = 1 − (1 − p_dev)^9` on every row of the wave (`smallcap` rows carry them for
reading; they are not in the family because they cannot graduate). Under the null with
independent rows, E[max dev z] = 1.49, P(any dev z ≥ 2) = 18.7%, P(any row passes the two-gate)
= 2.6%; with a ρ = 0.5 common factor, 12% and 2.1%.

##### Verdict algorithm (applied in this order to every dev row; the first rule that fires is the verdict)

1. Fewer than 120 monthly or 520 weekly valid dev periods: `insufficient`.
2. z_gate < 2.0, or net < 5 pp at base, or net ≤ 0 at high: `null`, written `null (underpowered
   below N pp)` when mde80_pp exceeds 12 pp.
3. Random-slice rank below 191 of 201: `null`.
4. Mirror slice long row at zero-cost z_gate ≥ 2.0: `artifact`, mirror excess recorded as bias
   size.
5. Any rule-4 condition fails, including the price-matched check: `artifact`.
6. Lag-250 zero-cost z_gate ≥ +2.0 (printed beside `actual_on_lag250_dates_z`): `characteristic`.
7. Else `confirm` for `uncapped`, `diagnostic-pass` for `smallcap` (non-graduating).

Confirm adds one step for `confirm` rows: the confirm gate above; under 30 monthly or 140 weekly
valid periods is `insufficient`, a miss is `fails confirm`. **`graduate` is not awarded in this
wave; its condition is locked now (C8):** a `confirm` row graduates only if the identical rule
re-run on delisting-complete Sharadar SEP plus SF1 DAILY (caps as of D, dev 1998–2020, confirm
2021–2023) keeps the sign, retains at least 50% of the free-data net excess at base and has
z_gate ≥ 2.0 in dev with the same confirm gate; step E records it. No purchase in this wave; the
Sharadar re-run is proposed, not bought.

##### What is interesting and what is not

Interesting in dev is the bar above (z_gate ≥ 2.0 at base, ≥ 5 pp net at base, > 0 at high,
≥ 120 / 520 periods) with every placebo and rule-4 condition passed: verdict `confirm` on
`uncapped`, which is the only thing that triggers a confirm-era run. Everything else is `null`,

`graduate` is not available in this wave (C8).

##### Decision rules (applied literally after the tables are written)

1. Dev tables are written and committed before any confirm-era run.
2. The confirm era is run only for `uncapped` rows whose dev verdict is `confirm`, with the
   identical rule; a `smallcap` row never reaches it.
3. If any row ends `confirm` and passes the confirm gate, the next action is the C8 re-run on
   delisting-complete data (a proposal to purchase Sharadar SEP + SF1 DAILY), not a build. If no
   row does, this entry says what the nulls rule out at their MDE80, and the program's next step is
   the owner's review per the proposal's section 9.
4. The holdout (2024 onward) is not downloaded and not read. No purchase.
5. The rank-v2 bar, the verdict algorithm, the three placebos, the `smallcap` lane rule and C8
   are recorded in `penumbra-specs` as a protocol amendment (a PR on that repo), in step E.
